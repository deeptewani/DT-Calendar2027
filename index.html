Planning notes

Monthly programs use the usual weekday rules. Jagannath Pooja takes place on the second Saturday of every month. Satyanarayan Katha follows the second-Sunday rule.

Janmashtami is listed on August 24 and 25 as alternatives requiring priest confirmation. Proposed dates include Bhagwat Katha, Kanwar Yatra, Mata Ki Chowki, the five-day Ganesha program and the Jagannath anniversary. Confirm all program times, muhurats and overlaps before publication.
